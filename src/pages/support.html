Common questions

What is free?

Five games stay free: m and a, t, p, apple, and car. Replay them as often as you like. Full Library is a one-time purchase for the remaining games and Milo’s Library crown. There is no subscription, and stars cannot be bought.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the Apple Account that bought Full Library. In LetterMilo, open the Grown-ups tab, choose See Full Library (or Purchase details), then Restore purchases. You need an internet connection.

Does restoring bring back progress?

Restoring a purchase unlocks Full Library. It does not restore games played, stars, or earned items. Those are saved on your device and may be in a device backup. Contact us before deleting the app if your progress will not open.

My saved play will not open. What can I do?

Close and reopen LetterMilo, then choose Try again. If that does not help, choose Grown-up help and contact us before deleting the app. In Grown-up help, Start fresh resets local play and keeps a copy of the old save on this device; it keeps your Full Library purchase.

Why isn’t speech working?

Speech needs a supported iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and microphone permission. Apple may need to download English language support first. Tapping activities are available too.

Does the app work offline?

Games and narration work offline. Speech may need a one-time language download. Buying and restoring Full Library need an internet connection.

Where is progress saved?

On your iPhone or iPad. Your device backups may also include it. Deleting the app’s data removes progress from that device.

When you email

Include your iPhone or iPad model, app version, and a short description of what happened. The version and build number are at the bottom of Grown-ups and in Saved play help. Please don’t send a child’s name or voice recordings.
